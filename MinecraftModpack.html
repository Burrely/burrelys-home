Burrely's Community Modpack

Here you'll find everything pertaining to my community modpack. Information, Instructions & Downloads.

If you're already familiar with installing .mrpack files on either Prism or Modrinth then you can go ahead and fetch the latest version of the modpack at the Changelog & Downloads section of this page.

Checklist

This checklist will make sure you're ready to play!

Modpack is NOT ready.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Download one of the following modpack launchers:
    • Prism
    • Modrinth
  2. Launch your modpack launcher and login with your Minecraft account.
  3. Download the latest version of the modpack at the Changelog & Downloads section of this page.
  4. Drag the downloaded .mrpack file into your launcher and let it download the mods.
  5. Congratulations, you have installed the modpack! All you have to do now is launch. The server will be visible in the multiplayer list!
